Quick answers

How do I play?

Tap the elf controls to move the two lifts between their fixed positions and catch the cakes. Cake collection and handoffs happen automatically when a lift is in position.

Oven release is automatic by default. Packed cakes fill Santa’s sleigh; each full delivery makes way for another sleigh while the same run continues. Missed or burned cakes cost lives.

Where are the sound and vibration settings?

Open Options to turn Music, Sound Effects or Vibration on or off. If you still cannot hear the game, check your device’s volume. Vibration also depends on your device’s support and settings.

How do I reset my best score?

Open Options, choose Reset High Score and confirm the reset. This resets the saved best score on that device.

Where can I find my app version?

Open Options and look for the small version number near the bottom-right of the screen. Please include both the version and build number when contacting support.

The game is not working as expected. What should I send?

Please describe what happened, what you expected and any steps that reproduce the issue. Your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and game version/build can help.

A screenshot is optional. Remove personal information from it before sending, and leave passwords, payment details and security codes out of your message.
