1. Who is responsible?

Wamballa Games is the trading name used by an individual based in the United Kingdom.

Steve Tyrell, trading as Wamballa Games, is responsible for the personal information described in this notice.

For support, privacy questions or a data-rights request, email wamballagamessupport@gmail.com.

In this notice, “we”, “us” and “our” mean that individual trading as Wamballa Games.

2. Information in the game

Santa’s Christmas Bakery stores your best score and game settings locally on your device. No account is required to play, and we do not operate a cloud-save or score-upload service.

You can reset your saved best score in Options. Device backups and restores are controlled by your device’s settings and platform provider.

3. When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email includes it, and the message and attachments you choose to send. This may include device details, an app version, screenshots or steps that explain a problem.

Providing this information is voluntary. We may not be able to answer or investigate without enough detail to understand your request. Please send only what is relevant.

Why we use it

We use support correspondence to reply, investigate problems and keep a proportionate record of how an enquiry was handled. The mailbox is used only for game support and privacy requests. We do not use these messages for marketing or routinely forward them to other people.

Our lawful basis

For ordinary support correspondence, our basis is legitimate interests: providing support for the game, resolving issues and keeping an appropriate record of enquiries, while respecting your rights. Where we must process information to comply with a legal duty, including handling a data-rights request, we rely on that legal obligation.

4. When you visit this website

The website is designed as a small set of static pages. It contains no added analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms or external font embeds.

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its own privacy statement describes its handling of service information. These hosting records are separate from the best score and preferences saved by the game.

Following a link to another website takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

5. Service providers and sharing

  • Google Gmail: we use a free personal Gmail account to receive, store and reply to support and privacy emails. Google handles information through this service under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the support and privacy pages.

We use support information for the purposes described above. We may disclose relevant information where necessary to meet a legal obligation or deal with a legal claim.

Read Google’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement for their own practices. See also GitHub Pages’ website-logging disclosure.

International processing

Google and GitHub operate internationally. Information handled by these services may be processed outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Google describes its use of adequacy decisions, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses where applicable in its data-transfer framework information. GitHub describes its international transfers and safeguards in its privacy statement. These links explain the providers’ arrangements; they do not mean we have a separate Google Workspace agreement.

6. How long do we keep information?

  • Best score and game preferences: remain in local app storage until reset, removed or affected by the device’s backup/restore settings.
  • Support correspondence: we delete messages and their attachments from our mailbox within 12 months after an enquiry is closed. We keep only relevant messages longer where necessary for an ongoing dispute or a legal obligation, and delete them when that reason ends.
  • Provider copies: Google may retain backup or service records for longer under its own retention practices. Deleting a message from our mailbox does not delete copies held by the sender.
  • Website hosting records: retention is governed by GitHub’s own practices; the 12-month support-email period does not apply to GitHub’s logs.

7. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis, UK data-protection law gives you rights to access your personal information, correct it, ask for erasure, restrict its use, object to processing and receive certain information in a portable form. These rights are not absolute.

To make a request, email wamballagamessupport@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably needed to confirm your identity and understand the request.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Visit ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint for information. You may contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern.

8. Children and families

The current game does not require a player account or a child’s contact details to play. A parent or guardian can use the support address to ask about a child’s information or help with the game. Please avoid sending a child’s personal information unless it is needed to explain the request.

9. Changes to this notice

We will update this page when the game or our handling of information changes. The notice will show its latest update date.

For any question, contact wamballagamessupport@gmail.com.
